## 👋️ About Me

I am a Research Associate at Heinrich Heine University Düsseldorf, a researcher at the Heine Center for Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (HeiCAD), and a Visiting Researcher at GESIS – Leibniz Institute for the Social Sciences.

My research focuses on Artificial Intelligence (AI), Natural Language Processing (NLP), Large Language Models (LLMs), and Generative AI (GenAI). I am particularly interested in how AI systems can learn to reason, understand language, and generalize across domains. My work spans computational argumentation, social media analysis, Conversational AI, and the development of intelligent systems capable of human-like reasoning and language understanding.

With more than five years of research experience, I have developed and evaluated models for NLP and speech-related applications.

My research has been published at leading venues such as ACL, NAACL, and LREC-COLING, and I actively contribute to the community as a reviewer for top-tier NLP conferences.

Beyond research, I enjoy mentoring students, developing educational content, and teaching Python programming and AI-related topics to make technology more accessible to broader audiences.

My long-term research vision is to contribute to the development of AI systems that move beyond pattern matching toward human-like reasoning, language abilities, and generalization across diverse domains and tasks.
